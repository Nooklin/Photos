Nooklin Photos is a photo and video gallery that stays on your device. It requests no internet permission, has no telemetry and does not use Google Play Services.

Features:
- Photo and video grid with a full-screen viewer
- Albums, favorites, screenshots, videos and panoramas
- Recently Deleted with 30-day expiry
- Hidden folder locked with your biometric or PIN
- Basic editor (rotate, flip)
- Info sheet with EXIF data
- Search by file name
- Optional on-device People and Pets grouping, off by default
- Light, dark or system theme

Open source under GPL-3.0.
